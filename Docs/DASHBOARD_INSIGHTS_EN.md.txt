# 📊 Dashboard Insights
Football Data Analysis 2024–2025

This document explains the analytical insights behind the visualizations and KPIs presented in the Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard analyzes football performance across three major European leagues:

⚽ Serie A  
⚽ Premier League  
⚽ La Liga  

Each page focuses on a specific analytical level, moving from league overview to player analysis and finally to a team case study.

---

# 🏟 1. Home – Project Navigation

The home page acts as the **central hub of the entire dashboard**.

The layout is designed as a football field to visually connect the project to the sport being analyzed.

From this page users can access the six analytical sections of the project:

⚽ Liga Analysis  
⚽ Serie A Analysis  
⚽ Premier League Analysis  
📊 League Comparison  
👟 Player Analysis  
🏟 Cagliari vs Napoli Case Study  

The purpose of this page is not analytical but **structural**: it guides the user through the data analysis journey.

The dashboard is structured to follow a logical analytical path:

League Analysis → Player Impact → League Comparison → Team Case Study.

📌 **Summary**

This page introduces the project and allows users to navigate the different analytical sections of the dashboard.

# 🇪🇸 2. Liga Analysis

This page analyzes the **overall performance of La Liga during the 2024–2025 season**.

The KPIs at the top of the page summarize the league's offensive production:

⚽ Total Goals  
📅 Total Matches  
📊 Average Goals per Match  

The data shows a league that combines offensive quality with tactical balance.

Barcelona clearly emerges as the most dominant team of the season.

🏆 Most Wins: Barcelona  
⚽ Best Attack: Barcelona  

Defensively, Athletic Bilbao stands out as the most solid team:

🧤 Best Defense: Athletic Bilbao

The player insights highlight the individual stars of the competition.

⚽ Top Scorer: Kylian Mbappé  
👟 Top Assist Provider: Lamine Yamal  
🧤 Best Save Percentage: Unai Simón

The **Top 10 Players chart** combines goals and assists to show which players have had the biggest impact on the league.

📌 **Summary**

This page provides a complete overview of La Liga, highlighting the most dominant teams and the players who have had the greatest influence on the competition.

# 📊 5. League Comparison

This page compares the three major European leagues analyzed in the project.

Several metrics are compared:

⚽ Goals per Match

# 🏟 7. Cagliari vs Napoli – Case Study

The final page presents a **direct comparison between two Serie A teams with very different seasonal objectives**.

⚽ Napoli — Title contender  
⚽ Cagliari — Relegation battle  

Several KPIs illustrate the performance gap between the two teams:

⚽ Goals Scored  
🧤 Goals Conceded  
👟 Assists  
📊 Expected Goals  
⏱ Minutes per Goal  

Napoli shows significantly higher attacking efficiency.

The team scores approximately **one goal every 58 minutes**, while Cagliari requires around **85 minutes per goal**.

Defensively, Napoli also performs better, conceding far fewer goals during the season.

The charts highlight the top scorers and top assist providers of both teams, identifying the players with the greatest influence on their squads.

📌 **Summary**

This page compares two teams with different competitive objectives and clearly shows how offensive efficiency and defensive stability influence overall team performance.
